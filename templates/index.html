Stage automation

When on, an opportunity's stage is set automatically from the furthest proof reached (done or in progress). A blocked opportunity always shows the blocked stage. Individual deals can still be overridden with "Set stage manually" in their edit form. Opportunities with no framework progress keep their current stage.
